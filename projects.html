Projects

Interactive tools built alongside research work. Each one loads its data directly in the browser — static builds, no backend.

Cities Growth Lab

A place-by-place read of municipal economies in the United States, Mexico and Great Britain: how population and wages have moved, where each place sits within its metropolitan labour market, and which places most resemble it.

cities.taimur.sh ↗ Vite · React · parquet in-browser
Morocco Cities Growth Lab

A spatial-equilibrium reading of Morocco's cities, 2014–2024. Cities are defined as labour markets rather than administrative units; the tool tracks migration, wages, employment composition and complexity, and asks whether each city's growth is constrained more by labour demand or labour supply. Bilingual (EN/FR).

morocco-cities.taimur.sh ↗ Vite · React · d3-geo
Global AEZ Yield Frontier Growth Lab

A country × agro-ecological zone typology for world agriculture, built on FAO GAEZ pixel data. Realised yields are benchmarked against a within-cluster frontier, so each comparison holds thermal regime and moisture band fixed rather than comparing across climates.

agri.taimur.sh ↗ Vite · React · Leaflet
Institutional Complexity Research

Economic-complexity tooling applied to V-Dem governance data. Countries are placed in an institutional space by the portfolio of institutional features they hold; the profile shows nearest institutional peers, the indicators driving that proximity, and country trajectories from 1975 to 2025.

institutions.taimur.sh ↗ Vite · React · Recharts
