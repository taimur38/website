Morocco Cities Interactive Report

2026

Harvard Growth Lab

A spatial-equilibrium reading of Morocco's cities, 2014–2024, published as an interactive report. We treat cities as labor markets, using functional urban areas estimated from satellite data from the GHSL rather than as administrative units. It tracks migration, wages, employment composition and economic complexity, and asks for each city whether growth is constrained more by labour demand or labour supply. Bilingual EN/FR.

Global AEZ Yield Frontier

2026

Harvard Growth Lab

Characterizing yield performance at the country × agro-ecological zone level, built on FAO GAEZ pixel data. Country-AEZs are clustered based on their AEZ characteristics and revealed crop-choice mixtures. Individual crop yields are then benchmarked against the best performer in the cluster, and then aggregated to generate a comparable yield ratio metric indicating distance from potential yield.

Pakistan Agricultural Productivity

2022

Harvard Kennedy School · Second Year Policy Analysis

A diagnostic of agricultural productivity in Pakistan, focusing on yield growth. The research identified poor yield performance as a central issue in Pakistani agriculture, and then develops two informative case studies - cotton, declining, and corn, on the rise - to tease out common underlying factors. It identifies seed quality as the main issue. The report then asks what market failures have prevented the private sector from improving seed quality separately, and concludes by recommending public seed quality competitions held by the government to resolve information failures.
